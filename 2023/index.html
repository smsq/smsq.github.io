Ninth Statistical Mechanics of Soft Matter Conference

The Ninth SM2 Meeting will be held at Murdoch University on 20-22 November, 2023, and will be followed by a one-day summer school on 23 November.



SM2 is a discussion meeting on all aspects of the equilibrium and nonequilibrium statistical mechanics of soft condensed matter, from fundamentals to applications, relating to liquids, colloids, polymers, gels, biological molecules, liquid crystals and other forms of soft matter.

This year the meeting returns to its traditional single-stream in-person format only.


Program, registration and title submissions

The preliminary program is available at this link.

The conference will start at 12 noon on Monday 20 November and finish at lunch time on Wednesday 22 November.

Summer School

We will hold a low-key summer school on Thursday 23 November, 2023, and will be designed with honours student, PhD students, and postdocs in mind. Please see the program for details.


Local information

The meeting will be held at the main campus of Murdoch University, on South Street.

The venue is “The Loft” located on the first floor of Sir Walter's restaurant (Club Murdoch) on Bush Court.

For public transport to Murdoch, check TransPerth for 20-minute bus connections to/from Fremantle (999/998) and 70-minute routes to/from airport. The bus stop at Murdoch is called "Discovery Way - Amenities" then walk up hill to Bush Court, or 25 minute walk from "Murdoch Station". Taxis from the airport take about 25 minutes and cost around $80.

If you come by car, the best car parks to use are P3 or P4 near the Chancellery Building. These have pay parking machines accepting cash and cards.

There are many accommodation option in Fremantle. A casual conference dinner will be held in Fremantle on Tuesday night at a restaurant to be confirmed.
